How a record flows

Two-step design: hard gates kill disqualifiers before any score exists, so a big number can never rescue an ineligible opportunity.

Hard gates7 kill-tests. Any fail → NO-GO (or Human Review where noted). No score is computed for a gated-out record.
Vendor / procurement layerDistrict-tier records and contract instruments only: vendor path + 8 procurement gates. Target-district carve-out routes here instead of the LEA-demote.
Weighted fit score10 dimensions, each 0–5, weights sum to 100. raw = Σ weight × score ÷ 5.
Confidence penaltyDeductions for unverified critical fields. final = max(0, raw − penalty). Unknown eligibility caps at QUALIFY.
RecommendationThresholds map the final score to GO TIER A · GO TIER B · QUALIFY · WATCH · NO-GO — plus overrides that no score can outvote.

Confidence penalty

Unknowns carry real operational cost. final = max(0, raw − penalty).

PenaltyCondition
−0primary source verified; all critical fields known
−6one critical field uncertain (amount, deadline, match, or geography)
−12two critical fields uncertain
−203+ uncertain, stale, or conflicting sources
Eligibility rule: UNKNOWN eligibility caps the recommendation at QUALIFY and applies -12, UNLESS the next action is a fast (<1 week) yes/no eligibility check. 'Likely eligible by applicant type' is NOT 'eligible' — capture the exact eligibility quote + the Relay fact needed to prove it.

Recommendation mapping

✕ NO-GO0–39 · Do not pursue
○ WATCH40–54 · Do not apply now; monitor or cultivate funder
◆ QUALIFY55–69 · Human review; seek clarification / partner / LOI-only path
● GO TIER B70–84 · Worth pursuing if staff capacity exists
● GO TIER A85+ · Pursue unless leadership overrides
OverridesAny failed hard gate => NO_GO (state which gate) · Any critical unknown => cap at QUALIFY (never GO) until verified · Low pursuit_readiness caps the rec regardless of fit (e.g. impossible deadline, missing required assets).

Funding instruments (9) — not interchangeable
  • discretionary_grant · cooperative_agreement · contract_procurement · cooperative_contract · formula_subgrant · scholarship_fellowship_pass_through · technical_assistance_center · prize · forecast
  • Co-op contract note: cooperative_contract = a purchasing-co-op vehicle (TIPS/OMNIA/BuyBoard/Sourcewell/AEPA) where ONE competitive award lets many member districts buy without their own RFP. Relay's warmest district-sell path is often 'get on co-op Y', not 'wait for district X's RFP'. Score the co-op award itself as the opportunity; note which target districts it reaches (districts.yaml coop_hooks).

Favoreducator prep · teacher residency · licensure certification pathways · school leadership · educator diversity · high need partnerships · outcomes evaluation DemoteDistrict-only / LEA-only / K-12-operator-only GRANT opps default to QUALIFY or NO_GO unless a credible partner/subrecipient role exists (set requires_partner=true). Review periodically; never assume the prefilter is lossless.
Partnership hypotheses (Stream 2) — a separate lane, never scored

Trigger: Discovered NOFO text requires/favors a high-need LEA partner AND joins (by geography/name/nces_lea_id) to >=1 config/districts.yaml target.

Label rule: Surface as `partnership_hypothesis` in a SEPARATE lane; never mix into the scored-opportunity table. Do NOT assign a GO/QUALIFY fit score — it is a cultivation signal, not a pursuable opp yet.

A hypothesis becomes a lead only when its feasibility fields resolve:

  • required_partner_type — e.g. "high-need LEA," "IHE + LEA consortium"
  • relay_allowed_role — prime | partner | subrecipient | vendor
  • mou_loi_requirement — is a signed MOU/LOI due with the application?
  • match_cost_share — required match and who carries it
  • cultivation_lead_time — months of relationship-building the NOFO implies
  • cultivate_by — explicit date = deadline − cultivation lead time
  • named_relationship_owner — who at Relay owns the district relationship (or "none — cold")

For the ~15 strategic accounts, attach the districts.yaml grant_posture_note (USASpending recipient history) so the hypothesis carries whether the district is an active/warm grantee (e.g. Chicago holds a direct TQP 84.336 — the strongest co-app signal in the set).

Digest output fields
  • Opportunity summary — 1-2 plain-language sentences: what it funds + who runs it. Shown as its own row.
  • Effort complexity — Low | Medium | High | Very High — application COMPLEXITY, not staff-hours. Low = short LOI / simple form; Medium = narrative + budget; High = + partners/evaluation/data; Very High = + mandatory match, MOUs, multiple components.
  • Funding type — grant | cooperative_agreement | contract | scholarship_pass_through | formula. Flags money Relay can't use as flexible operating support — e.g., scholarship pass-through (Noyce funds student scholarships, not org operations) or cooperative agreements with heavy federal involvement.
  • Past winners — Recent notable recipients under this Assistance Listing (CFDA), via USASpending. CAVEAT: broad CFDAs (e.g. 47.076 = ALL of NSF EHR) span many programs — label as approximate and link the USASpending drill-down for the specific program. Specific CFDAs (e.g. 84.325) are reliable.
  • Recurrence — If a recurring program, note the typical cycle + predicted next deadline so WATCH items have a return date.
Pitfall guards — why the two-step design exists
  • Big award, poor fit -> mission_program_fit is top weight + mission_boundary is a hard gate.
  • 'Education' treated as auto-relevant -> require a specific Relay-aligned use, not broad ed language.
  • Missed cost-share -> match is BOTH a hard gate and a scored burden.
  • Applying too close to deadline -> deadline_feasibility gate + effort_deadline_runway dimension.
  • Chasing cold hypercompetitive federal -> win_probability + funder_relationship reduce score.
  • Optimistic assumptions on unknowns -> confidence_penalty + HUMAN_REVIEW block false GO.
  • Confusing contract/RFP revenue with grants -> source_type field forces different treatment.
